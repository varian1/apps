## What it does

Text Toolkit is one screen with nine text tools. Type or paste text into the box at the top, pick a tool, and copy or share the result. It counts words, changes letter case, cleans up spacing, sorts lines, finds and replaces, reverses, encodes, formats JSON and compares two texts.

- Every tool runs on your device and works without internet.
- No account. Your text is never uploaded, and the app does not use your camera, photos, contacts or location.
- Your text is not saved. It is gone when you close the app.
- Free. A banner ad may show at the bottom of the screen.

## Get started

1. Open Text Toolkit. The **YOUR TEXT** box holds a short sample so you can try each tool.
2. Tap **Clear** and type your own text, or tap **Paste** to replace the text with what you copied in another app. **Sample** puts the sample text back.
3. Pick a tool in the row under the box: **Counter**, **Case**, **Clean**, **Lines**, **Replace**, **Reverse**, **Encode**, **Format** or **Diff**. Swipe the row sideways to see them all.

![Main screen: the YOUR TEXT box with Paste, Sample and Clear, the row of tools, and the Counter numbers](shots/01-home.png)

## Count words and characters

1. Tap **Counter**.
2. Read the numbers: **Characters**, **No spaces**, **Words**, **Sentences**, **Paragraphs**, **Lines** and **Reading time**.
3. Look at **TOP WORDS** below to see the words you used most, up to eight, each with its count.

The numbers change as you type. Reading time is based on 200 words a minute.

## Change the letter case

1. Tap **Case**.
2. Scroll the cards. Each one shows your text in another style: **UPPERCASE**, **lowercase**, **Title Case**, **Sentence case**, **Capitalized Case**, **aLtErNaTiNg**, **iNVERSE**, **camelCase**, **PascalCase**, **snake_case**, **kebab-case**, **CONSTANT_CASE** and **URL slug**.
3. Tap **Copy** on the card you want.

![Case tool: cards for UPPERCASE, lowercase and Title Case, each with Use input, Share and Copy](shots/02-case.png)

## Clean up text

1. Tap **Clean**.
2. Tap one action:
   - **Clean all**: removes tabs, repeated spaces, spaces at the ends of lines, empty lines and repeated lines in one go.
   - **Extra spaces**, **Trim lines**, **No empty lines**, **No duplicates** or **Remove tabs**: does only that one thing.
3. Read the result under **CLEANED RESULT**. Your own text in the box stays as it was.

![Clean tool: the six cleaning buttons and the CLEANED RESULT card](shots/03-clean.png)

## Sort or reverse

1. Tap **Lines** to work on whole lines: **A to Z**, **Z to A**, **Reverse lines**, **Shortest first**, **No duplicates** or **Reverse characters**. Sorting ignores capital letters and spaces at the start of a line.
2. Tap **Reverse** to turn text around: **Entire text**, **Each word**, **Word order**, **Line order** or **Characters per line**.
3. The result shows in the card below the buttons.

## Find and replace

1. Tap **Replace**.
2. Type the text to look for in **Find** and the new text in **Replace with**.
3. Tap the options to turn them on or off: **Replace all** (off changes only the first match), **Case sensitive** and **Whole word**.
4. Check the number of replacements and the **REPLACEMENT PREVIEW**. Both update as you type.

![Replace tool: Find and Replace with fields, the three option buttons, the replacement count and the preview](shots/04-replace.png)

The search looks for exactly what you type. It does not use patterns or wildcards.

## Encode, decode and format

1. Tap **Encode**, choose **URL**, **Base64** or **HTML Entities**, then tap **Encode** or **Decode**.
2. Tap **Format** and choose **Format JSON** to indent JSON with two spaces, **Minify JSON** to put it on one line, or **Strip Markdown** to turn Markdown into plain text.
3. If the JSON has a mistake, a line near the top of the screen starts with "Invalid JSON" and says what is wrong.

## Compare two texts

1. Put the first version in **YOUR TEXT** and tap **Diff**.
2. Type or paste the second version into **COMPARE WITH**.
3. Read **DIFF COMPARISON**. Lines only in the second version are green with a **+**. Lines only in the first version are red with a **-**. Lines that match have no mark.
4. Tap **Copy Diff** to copy the comparison with its + and - marks.

![Diff tool: the COMPARE WITH box and the DIFF COMPARISON card with one green added line and one red removed line](shots/05-diff.png)

## Copy, share or keep working

1. Tap **Copy** on a result card to put it on the clipboard.
2. Tap **Share** to send it to another app.
3. Tap **Use input** to move the result into **YOUR TEXT**, so you can run another tool on it.

## Questions

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. All nine tools run on your phone. Only ads use a network connection.

**Is my text saved?**
No. The app keeps your text only while it is open. Copy or share a result you want to keep.

**Why is the result empty after I switched tools?**
Clean, Lines, Reverse, Encode and Format share one result card, and it is cleared when you change tool. Tap the action again. Do the same after you edit your text. Counter, Case, Replace and Diff update by themselves.

**Does it follow dark mode?**
Yes. The app uses the light or dark theme set on your phone.

**Where are the licenses?**
Scroll to the bottom and tap **Open-source licenses**. **Privacy policy** is next to it.

## Privacy

## What it does

Steady is a calm habit tracker. You choose the days each habit is due, check in with one tap and watch your streaks grow. It is a wellness tool for building routines, not a medical app.

- No account and no cloud sync. Your habits and check-ins are stored on your phone.
- Streaks count only the days you schedule, so a planned day off does not break them.
- Optional reminders are set on your device.
- Free for up to 3 habits. Premium adds unlimited habits, full history, more stats and colors, and removes ads.

## Get started

1. Open Steady. The **Today** tab shows "Start your first habit".
2. Tap **Create a habit**.
3. Type a name and tap **Save**. The habit is due every day unless you change its schedule.

Steady asks to send notifications only when you turn on a reminder.

![Today tab with the progress card, three habits under Due today and their check buttons](shots/01-home.png)

## Create a habit

1. On **Today**, tap the **+** button in the top right.
2. Under **Name**, type what you want to do, up to 40 characters.
3. Pick an **Icon** and a **Color**.
4. Under **Schedule**, tap **Daily**, **Weekdays** or **Weekends**, or tap single days to turn them on or off. At least one day must stay on.
5. Tap **Save**. Tap **Cancel** to leave without saving.

![New Habit form with Name, Icon, Color and Schedule, and Save in the top right](shots/02-new-habit.png)

## Check in

1. Open **Today**. Habits scheduled for today are under **Due today**.
2. Tap the round button next to a habit to mark it done. Tap it again to undo.
3. Watch the card at the top count up, for example "2 of 3 done", until it says "All done for today".

Your streak shows under each habit name. Habits that are not scheduled today are listed under **Not due today** with their days.

## Set a reminder

1. Open a habit, tap **Edit** and scroll to **Reminder**. You can also do this while creating a habit.
2. Turn on **Daily reminder** and allow notifications if Android asks.
3. Set the time: the minus and plus buttons move it by one hour, **−5 min** and **+5 min** by five minutes. Or tap one of the four preset times.
4. Tap **Save**.

The reminder is a notification on the days the habit is scheduled. If notifications are turned off for Steady, the form tells you and shows **Open Settings**.

## See streaks and history

1. Tap a habit on **Today** or **Stats**.
2. Read **Current streak**, **Longest streak** and **Last 30 days**.
3. Under **History**, look at the month: **Done**, **Missed** and **Upcoming** days are marked. Use the arrows to change month.
4. Tap a past scheduled day to mark it done if you forgot to check in, or tap it again to undo.

![Habit screen with Current streak, Longest streak, Last 30 days and the History calendar](shots/03-habit.png)

The free version shows this month and last month.

## Check your stats

1. Tap the **Stats** tab.
2. **Last 7 days** and **Last 30 days** show how many scheduled check-ins you completed, as a percentage.
3. Under **Streaks**, each habit shows its current streak and its best one. Tap a habit to open it.

![Stats tab with Last 7 days, Last 30 days and the Streaks list](shots/04-stats.png)

## Edit, archive or delete a habit

1. Tap a habit, then tap **Edit** to change its name, icon, color, schedule or reminder. Tap **Save**.
2. Tap **Archive habit** to hide it from Today and Stats and keep its check-ins. To bring it back, open **Settings** and tap **Restore** under **Archived habits**.
3. Tap **Delete habit** to remove the habit and all its check-ins for good. This cannot be undone.

## Change the color and settings

1. Tap the **Settings** tab.
2. Under **Appearance**, tap an accent color. **Ocean** and **Forest** are free.
3. Under **Privacy**, you can turn on **Do not sell or share my personal information**.
4. **Version** is at the bottom, under **About**.

Steady follows the light or dark theme set on your phone.

![Settings tab with the Premium card, Appearance colors, Data and Privacy](shots/05-settings.png)

## Premium

Steady Premium is bought inside the app through your store account. Open **Settings** and tap **Upgrade** to see the plans and prices before you pay. It gives you:

- Unlimited habits. The free version includes 3.
- Your full history and **Advanced stats**: completion by weekday, and which habits are most consistent or need attention.
- All accent colors.
- **Export data**: a JSON file of all your habits and check-ins, saved to a folder you pick.
- No ads. Free users may see one small banner on **Stats**.

If you bought Premium before, tap **Restore purchases** in **Settings**.

## Questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. There is no sign-up and no cloud sync. Your habits stay on your phone.

**Does a day off break my streak?**
No. Streaks count only scheduled days. Today also does not break a streak until the day is over.

**I forgot to check in yesterday. Can I fix it?**
Yes. Open the habit and tap the day under **History**.

**What happens if I uninstall the app?**
Your habits are stored inside the app on your phone, so they are removed with it. Premium can export a copy first.

**Is Steady a medical app?**
No. It helps you build routines. It does not measure, diagnose or treat any health condition.

## Privacy

Your habits and check-ins are stored on your phone. There is no account and no cloud sync, and Steady does not send your habit data to any server. The free version may show ads; you can review your ad choices under **Privacy** in **Settings**.

What it does

Currency Converter turns one amount into all your saved currencies at once. It is made for travellers, online shoppers and anyone who needs a quick conversion. It keeps the last rates it downloaded, so you can still convert without a connection.

  • Convert one amount into many currencies at the same time.
  • Search more than 150 currencies by name or code.
  • Use the built-in calculator, then send the result to the converter.
  • Pick your rate source, choose a light or dark theme, and use the app in 16 languages.

Get started

  1. Open the app while you have a connection. It downloads the latest rates and saves them on your phone.
  2. If the app asks for consent for ads, make your choice. You can change it later in Settings.
  3. Open the Convert tab and type an amount.

Convert an amount

  1. Open the Convert tab.
  2. Type an amount.
  3. Read the result in each of your saved currencies.
  4. To convert from a different currency, tap that currency.

The app always shows where the rates come from, the rate date and when they were downloaded.

Save your favourite currencies

  1. Open the currency list from the Convert tab.
  2. Search for a currency by name or code.
  3. Save it as a favourite.
  4. Put your favourites in the order you want.

Use the calculator

  1. Open the Calculator tab.
  2. Work out your sum. You can add, subtract, multiply, divide, and use brackets and percentages.
  3. Send the result to the converter.

Choose a rate source

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Pick one of the three free rate sources.
  3. Test the source to check that it can be reached.

Sources differ in coverage and update times. Most update once a day.

Change the theme

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Choose light or dark, or let the app follow your phone.

Questions

Does it work offline? Yes, once the app has downloaded rates at least once. After that it uses the last saved rates when you're offline or a rate source can't be reached.

Are these live rates? No. They are reference rates, and most sources update once a day. The app shows the rate date so you know how fresh they are.

Why does my bank give me a different amount? Banks, card networks, money-transfer services and exchange offices use their own rates and fees. Use the app's rates for information only.

Why are some currencies missing? Each rate source covers a different set of currencies, and some cover far fewer. Try another source in Settings.

Does it show crypto or gold? No. The app shows world currencies only.

Privacy

Your amounts and saved currencies stay on your phone, and the app contacts the rate source you choose to get rates. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location and app interactions; where the law requires it, you're asked for consent first and can change your choice in Settings. A privacy policy is not published yet.
